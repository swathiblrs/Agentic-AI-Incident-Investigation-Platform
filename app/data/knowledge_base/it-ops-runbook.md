---
title: IT Operations Incident Runbook
source: IT Operations Runbook IT-001
tags: it, vpn, email, endpoint, identity, access
---

IT operations incidents should confirm impacted users, affected access path, device posture, identity provider
status, VPN concentrator health, email service status, DNS resolution, and network reachability.

Response should include scope confirmation, workaround guidance, service-owner escalation, and a user-facing
communication update when many users are affected.
